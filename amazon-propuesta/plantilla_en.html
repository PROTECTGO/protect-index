WHAT WE DO FOR YOU

1. We handle your account setup

USD {{setup_precio}}, a one-time charge for this service. Regular price: USD {{setup_precio_regular}}. This promotional price is tied to signing up for our load management for {{compromiso_dias}} days, with at least one truck.

We help you gather and review your information, prepare and submit the application, and follow up on Amazon’s responses. You provide the documents and complete any validations that must be done in your name.

Amazon decides whether to approve the account. The USD {{setup_precio}} pays for our management work. It does not buy or guarantee approval.

2. We manage your loads and your operation

Once your account is enabled to operate, the Full Dispatch service includes:

  • Reviewing your performance score and filing disputes with evidence when appropriate.
  • Reviewing results and opportunities to improve your operation.

The service already includes score support and light operational control. Levels 1 and 2 are not billed separately in this package.

Load availability, contracts, payments, and score decisions depend on Amazon. We do not promise any specific amount of income.

Contingencies

This proposal is contingent on receiving and validating: the client’s complete documents; any validations Amazon requires in the client’s name; and the company’s eligibility with Amazon (active authority, current insurance, and an acceptable safety rating). This proposal does not confirm the company’s eligibility with Amazon.

Notices

This proposal is valid for 30 days from its issue date. Amazon decides account approval, load availability, payments, and score decisions; ProtectGo does not promise any specific amount of income. The setup fee pays for the management work, not for approval. This proposal does not include insurance, repairs, or other truck operating expenses.

Client responsibilities

Report every incident on the same day it occurs, whether or not you are at fault, so evidence can be gathered in time; keep access to your Relay account; provide proof of delivery and documents when requested; and notify us of pauses, unit changes, or driver changes before they happen.

Billing and payment

The commission is settled weekly in arrears by authorized ACH debit. The weekly minimum applies as long as the unit remains assigned to the service. The minimum commitment is {{compromiso_dias}} days. Late payment entitles ProtectGo to suspend the service and, if it persists, to send the debt to collections.
